### Core Engineering Domains

**Cloud Native & Kubernetes**
* **Orchestration:** Kubernetes (Bare-Metal & Cloud), VMware Tanzu (TKG), K3s
* **eBPF & Networking:** Cilium CNI, Hubble Observability, XDP, eBPF Service Mesh
* **Observability & IaC:** Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry, Helm, Terraform, Ansible

**Cybersecurity & Runtime Defense**
* **Kernel & Runtime:** Falco (Modern eBPF driver, syscall filtering, custom rulesets)
* **Perimeter & Zero Trust:** Fortinet FortiGate, FortiManager, SD-WAN, IPS/IDS
* **Hardening & Auditing:** CIS Benchmarks, NIST 800-53, Linux Kernel Hardening

**Enterprise Virtualization & Hybrid Cloud**
* **Hypervisor:** VMware vSphere 7/8, ESXi, vCenter Server
* **Software-Defined Network:** VMware NSX-T (Microsegmentation, Overlay)
* **Storage & Resiliency:** vSAN, High Availability (HA), DRS, Fault Tolerance

**Linux Systems & Kernel Engineering**
* **OS & Kernel:** Debian, Ubuntu Server, Rocky Linux, RHEL, `sysctl` TCP/IP stack tuning, `cgroups v2`, namespaces
* **Automation & Scripting:** Go (Golang), Python, Bash, C/C++, PowerShell
* **Network Analysis:** TCP/IP Protocols, Wireshark, BGP, OSPF, VLANs
